Who We Are

L’abricot Labs is an independent technology studio focused on the research, design, and development of proprietary digital products.

Our work centers on building internal software platforms and applications, with an emphasis on thoughtful design, scalable systems, and long-term product development. We operate as a product-driven company, dedicating our efforts to initiatives developed and maintained in-house.

Rooted in experimentation, craft, and technical rigor, we explore new ideas at the intersection of software, design, and emerging technology. Select projects are currently in active development and will be shared publicly at a later time.

Aaron Silverstein
Founder & Principal

Aaron is a product strategist with a background in UX design, research, and software development. He has led and contributed to the design and development of complex digital products across consumer and technology-focused organizations. He founded L’abricot Labs as a product-driven technology studio focused on the research, design, and development of proprietary software products. His work emphasizes long-term product strategy, thoughtful systems design, and building scalable digital platforms from the ground up.
